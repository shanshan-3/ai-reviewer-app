# AI-Powered Interactive Reviewer — Project Overview

## Project Title
An AI-Powered Interactive Reviewer for BSIT Students of Pangasinan State University

## Overview
The proposed system is a centralized, AI-powered, interactive, and adaptive reviewer designed for Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students at Pangasinan State University – Asingan Campus. It aims to make examination preparation more structured, personalized, and effective by generating IT-specific questions, providing immediate explanations and feedback, adapting difficulty to student performance, and tracking learning progress.

## Problem
Students rely on scattered online resources and static study notes because they lack a centralized review application tailored to their IT curriculum. This makes preparation time-consuming and makes it difficult to identify technical learning gaps and assess readiness for examinations. Instructors also face the time-consuming task of preparing targeted quizzes for different IT subjects.

## Proposed Solution
A web/mobile-accessible review application that combines AI-generated quizzes, interactive flashcards, personalized recommendations, adaptive question difficulty, instant corrective feedback, and progress tracking to help students focus on areas where they need improvement.

## Target Users
- BSIT students of Pangasinan State University – Asingan Campus
- IT department faculty and instructors at the campus

## Main Goal
Provide an intelligent, automated, and interactive reviewer that dynamically generates IT-specific questions, gives real-time feedback, and adapts difficulty according to each student's performance.

## Selected Features
1. **Student Login:** Secure access and student profiles.
2. **AI Quiz Generator:** Customized multiple-choice and coding-syntax quizzes based on uploaded course materials or selected topics.
3. **Smart Flashcards:** Interactive cards for reviewing technical terms and definitions.
4. **Real-Time AI Feedback:** Explanations of incorrect answers to reinforce understanding.
5. **Student Progress Dashboard:** Tracking for scores, study streaks, and subject mastery.
6. **Performance Reports:** In-app reports showing strengths and weaknesses across IT subjects.

## Design Thinking Approach
- **Empathize:** Understand students' study habits and challenges through interviews, surveys, and observations.
- **Define:** Identify the lack of a centralized, personalized, interactive IT reviewer as the core problem.
- **Ideate:** Explore AI-generated quizzes, flashcards, adaptive learning, instant feedback, and analytics.
- **Prototype:** Create an interface and system model illustrating the student review experience.

## Proposed User Flow
1. Student logs in.
2. Student opens the dashboard.
3. Student configures and generates a quiz.
4. Student answers questions in a live quiz session.
5. System provides AI feedback and explanations.
6. Student sees quiz results and diagnostic information.
7. Student reviews progress and performance trends.

**Simplified flow:** Login → Dashboard → Quiz Generator → Quiz → AI Feedback → Results → Progress

## Intended Outcome
Students can prepare for examinations using a single interactive review platform instead of scattered materials, receive guidance on weaker topics, and monitor their learning progress. Instructors may benefit from reduced manual work when preparing targeted assessments.
